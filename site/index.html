10 PM She settles in.

You're typing; she kneads along. You scroll; she paws at the page rolling by. Stroke her head and she purrs. Ignore her completely and she'll wash her face, wrap her tail around her paws, and doze off. She's a cat, not a notification.

11:52 PM You think out loud.

Press ⌃⌥Space and just say it. “Remind me to send the invoice at four.” “Note: pricing needs a floor.” “What's the flag for force-with-lease?” She works out what you meant, shows you chips, and waits three seconds before doing anything at all. Watch her run one, down there ↘

  • Reminders that come back

    The 4 PM reminder you ignored returns at 4:30 with choices (DONE+30MTONIGHTDROP), and once more after that. Then she lets it be. She never nags.

  • An evening wind-down

    Around 6:30 she offers to sweep whatever's left to tomorrow at nine. One question, once per evening, only if something's actually due.

  • Undo follows everything

    Every to-do, note, and capture leaves an eight-second ADDED — TAP TO UNDO bubble behind it. Tap it and it never happened. Notes land as markdown in your folder.

2 AM Your agents are still working.

Claude Code in one terminal, Codex in another. She wears a tiny LED so you can stop checking on them.

  • Working

    An amber breath while an agent thinks. Hover it: who's running, doing what, since when.

  • Done

    One hop per finished turn. Background pipelines skip the party and file straight into her inbox, so a bursty automation never gets loud.

  • Needs you

    Agent questions arrive as chips on her. Click an answer and she jumps to the right terminal tab and types it in for you.

  • Spoken to

    “Tell claude to run the tests.” She finds the session, asks you first, types it into the prompt, and leaves the Enter key to you.

7 AM She's glad you're back.

A good-morning with what's due today. Some days, a treasure she found overnight, set neatly at her feet. And if you've been gone a while, one bubble with everything you missed (2 agent runs · 1 ask · 1 due) that opens her inbox when you tap it.

Weekends never break your streak. A week away doesn't earn you a lecture. Coming back is always a happy thing.

House rules.

A creature with a permanent spot on your screen has to deserve it. Hers are non-negotiable:

She asks first.
Chips before anything happens. Typing into your terminal always takes a click. Nothing is ever auto-sent.
Undo follows everything.
Eight seconds to take it back: the to-do, the note, the capture. Tap and it never happened.
No guilt. Ever.
Missed reminders don't escalate; she stops asking after twice. Absence gets a reunion, not a report card.
Your machine, your words.
Voice is transcribed on-device. The optional brain has a daily cap with a visible meter. Notes are plain markdown files in your folder.
Nothing phones home.
There's no account to create and no telemetry to opt out of. She's a local process talking to herself on 127.0.0.1.
Readable to the last pixel.
Open source, and every frame of her is ASCII art in a single file. You can read her entire anatomy.
